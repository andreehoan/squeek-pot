01What is Squeek Pot?

A no-loss lottery for SQK. Deposit, and your SQK stays 100% yours — withdraw every token whenever you want. While it sits in the pot, your deposit size is your odds of winning a prize that's drawn once a day.

You can't lose your deposit. The only thing ever paid out is a separate prize pot that sponsors seed — never your principal, and never anyone else's. Win or lose, you keep what you put in.

The winner each day is picked by Chainlink VRF 2.5 — on-chain, provably-fair randomness that nobody (not even the team) can predict or rig.

02How to play

  • Connect your wallet on Ethereum Sepolia (testnet).
  • Deposit SQK. Approve once, then deposit. Minimum position is 1 SQK. Your balance = your tickets.
  • Wait for the daily draw. Every 24 hours the wheel spins and one depositor wins the whole prize pot.
  • Withdraw anytime. Pull out any amount, in full — even while the game is paused or a draw is mid-flight. A partial withdrawal must leave either nothing or at least 1 SQK behind.
WHO FUNDS THE PRIZE? The prize is seeded by the team and sponsors (via the permissionless fundPrize) — and a future, separately-audited version routes Squeekchain staking yield into the prize automatically, so the pot funds itself. It is never taken from deposits; deposits stay 100% withdrawable either way.

03Your odds

Odds are simply your share of all deposits:

win chance = your deposit ÷ total deposits

Deposit more, get more tickets. There's no edge from splitting across wallets — 100 SQK in one wallet or across ten wallets is the same total weight. The live odds shown in the app are your raw deposit share; if you hold a Genesis NFT your real draw weight is higher (next section).

04Genesis NFT boost

Hold at least one Squeek Genesis NFT and your ticket weight is multiplied by 1.2 (+20%) when the draw takes its snapshot. One NFT is enough — holding more doesn't stack.

  • Fixed & equal. Every holder gets the same +20%. It's immutable — baked in at deploy, nobody can crank it, not even the owner.
  • Still no-loss. The boost only changes the odds weighting. Your deposit is as safe and withdrawable as anyone's.
  • Can't be gamed. The boost multiplies your deposit, so splitting SQK across many NFT wallets gives no extra edge.
  • A nudge, not a trump card. +20% is bounded — a bigger non-holder deposit still beats a small holder. Money dominates; the NFT tilts a close race.
HOW BIG IS +20%, REALLY? Head-to-head with an equal non-holder deposit, a holder wins ~54.5% of the time vs 45.5%. Put another way: each 1 SQK a holder deposits is worth 1.2 tickets instead of 1.0. Small, fair edge — enough to reward holding, not enough to make deposits pointless.

05The daily draw

The draw is fully permissionless — no admin pulls the lever. It runs in three steps:

  • Request — once the 24h timer is up, anyone (or the automatic keeper) calls requestDraw. This freezes the snapshot — the exact player set and weights for this round — and asks Chainlink VRF for a random number.
  • Randomness lands — Chainlink VRF 2.5 delivers the random word on-chain a few blocks later.
  • Settle — anyone (or the keeper) calls settleDraw; the weighted winner is picked from the frozen snapshot and paid the prize instantly.
WHY THE SNAPSHOT MATTERS Because the player set is frozen when the draw starts, nobody can watch the random number arrive and then deposit to grab the win — late deposits and withdrawals only count toward the next round. The prize amount is locked in at request time too.

An automatic keeper triggers both steps on schedule, so draws happen on time even if no player lifts a finger. If Chainlink ever failed to deliver, the round can be safely reset (prize rolls to the next day) — see trust & safety.

06Why it's truly no-loss

Deposits and the prize live in two separate accounts inside the contract:

  • Total deposits — the sum of everyone's SQK. The contract never pays a prize out of this.
  • Prize reserve — sponsor-seeded SQK. A draw only ever transfers from here.

Because the SQK held by the contract is always at least the total of all deposits, every depositor can always withdraw in full — withdrawals aren't even pausable. It's the first thing an auditor should check, and it holds by construction.

07Trust & safety

What the owner can do is deliberately tiny, and none of it touches your money:

  • Tune VRF plumbing (gas lane, confirmations) — only while no draw is in flight. No effect on funds, odds, or timing.
  • Pause new deposits and new draw requests. Withdrawals and settling an in-flight draw stay enabled — a pause can never trap your SQK.
  • Unstick a dead draw — if Chainlink never delivered, after 1 day the owner can reset the round. Funds are untouched; the prize rolls forward.

What the owner cannot do: take or move deposits, touch the prize, change anyone's odds, change the +20% boost, swap which NFT counts, or pick the winner.

  • Rig-proof randomness. The VRF coordinator is pinned at deploy — a swapped coordinator can neither start nor finish a draw.
  • Lost-key safety. If the owner key vanished, anyone can reset a genuinely stuck draw after 7 days, so draws can't freeze forever.
  • Reentrancy-guarded throughout, and a misbehaving NFT contract can't brick a draw (the holder-check is gas- and output-bounded).
TOKEN PRECONDITION The no-loss promise assumes SQK is a standard ERC-20 — fixed-supply, non-rebasing, no fee-on-transfer, no transfer hooks. Squeekchain's SQK meets this. The contract is not safe to repoint at a non-standard token without a re-audit.

09FAQ

Can I lose my deposit?

No. Prizes are paid only from the separate sponsor-funded reserve. Your deposit is always 100% withdrawable.

Do I need the Genesis NFT to play?

No — anyone can deposit and win. The NFT is an optional +20% odds boost, nothing more.

Who runs the draw?

Nobody in particular — it's permissionless. An automatic keeper fires it on schedule, but any player can trigger it too.

Could the team rig who wins?

No. Winners come from Chainlink VRF 2.5 over a snapshot frozen before the random number is known, and the VRF coordinator is pinned so it can't be swapped. The owner has no winner-picking power.

What's the minimum, and how many can play?

Minimum position is 1 SQK; up to 500 depositors can be in the pot at once.

What happens to late deposits right before a draw?

They count toward the next round. The current round's players and prize are locked in the moment the draw is requested.
